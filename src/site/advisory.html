The lifecycle

Where each service fits.

Most programmes need different support at different stages. The Capital Programme Health Check can be commissioned at any point; knowledge work closes the loop from handover back to the next business case.

01 · Capital Delivery

Independent assurance for capital programmes.

For sponsors, boards and programme directors who need an independent, client-side view of a project or programme.

Where work takes place around an operating railway or occupied asset, the review considers how the programme manages risks to people and operations, who owns those risks, and what evidence is needed before handover.

Request a proposal

What the Capital Delivery service covers

  • Capital Programme Health Check: independent review of scope, cost, schedule and risk
  • Risk and opportunity assessment
  • Governance and assurance review
  • Executive findings report
  • Prioritised corrective action plan
What you get
A clear view of where the programme stands, what is driving risk, and which actions matter most, written for decision-makers.
Lifecycle stage
Business case to handover, at any stage

How engagements work

Scope, proposal, deliver, handover.

Every engagement starts with an agreed scope and a written proposal. Any software development or implementation is scoped separately.

01

Scope

A short conversation to understand the decision, the programme and what a useful answer looks like.

02

Proposal

A written proposal setting out scope, approach, deliverables, timing and commercial terms.

03

Deliver

Document review, interviews and site visits as agreed, with regular check-ins and no surprises.

04

Handover

Findings, registers and recommendations handed over in a form your team can use and maintain.

Discuss an engagement.

Describe the programme or decision and the kind of view you need. You will receive a written proposal before any work begins.
