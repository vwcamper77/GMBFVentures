About Gavin

Infrastructure advice grounded in delivery.

Gavin Ferns founded GMBF Ventures to advise on rail infrastructure and capital programmes. He brings 27 years of experience across rail, property and construction, including stations and depots.

He helps teams make investment and delivery decisions, plan work around live operations and prepare assets for safe handover. He also develops practical digital tools, including SnagSort for managing construction defects through to closeout.
