Local AI for contract review

Help project teams find the obligations that govern their work, using AI designed to run on infrastructure they control.

Area
Applied AI
Workflow
Contract and policy review
Technology
Ollama, Open WebUI, Python
Stage
Prototype and business case

Download the 2-page PDF

About this case study. This project reached prototype and business-case stage. The team verified local model discovery and tested individual components. Local inference and browser-to-model integration still needed work. The project produced a business case and a proposed 12-week route to a tested MVP.

The problem

The answer is rarely in one document.

One priority contract ran to around 200 pages and had around 50 related policies. Project managers, engineers and operational staff needed to find the right provision before acting on questions about notices, design responsibilities or payment.

Finding the right wording matters when a missed notice or an unclear responsibility can change who carries the risk.

The approach

Start with one contract and its policies.

Gavin Ferns scoped a browser-based assistant that could take a plain-English question, cross-reference approved documents and return an answer with sources. The prototype used Ollama for local model hosting, Open WebUI for the browser interface and Python for contract and policy processing.

How it would work

Who must receive a notice, and by when?

An illustrative workflow for the proposed MVP, using a fictional question.

The question

A project manager needs to know who must receive a notice, what starts the clock and when the notice is due.

The proposed answer

Bring the notice clause, recipient schedule and any relevant amendment together, with references to the source documents.

The review

The commercial reviewer checks the signed documents, resolves any conflicting wording and confirms what the team should do.

What it is designed to change

Easier access to the governing wording.

These are the intended benefits. A production pilot would test answer accuracy, source references and time saved.
