Capability case study

Ask your drawings and contracts

SnagSort Project Intelligence is designed to help construction teams find the right drawings and contract clauses, with links to the original sources.

About this case study. GMBF implemented drawing relationships and document indexing, and developed and tested parts of the Ask SnagSort interface. The full workflow across drawings and contracts still requires end-to-end production validation.

The problem

One issue can span several documents.

A question about a ticket office door can send a project manager from the general arrangement drawing to a door schedule, an installation detail and the contract. Each document can have its own revisions and references to follow.

The project manager needs the correct documents and revisions before deciding what to inspect or asking the commercial team what the contract requires.

The approach

Connect the documents before answering.

GMBF extended SnagSort to store drawing sheets, revisions, cross-references and project locations alongside indexed passages from contracts and specifications. The assistant is designed to retrieve relevant material first and link its answer back to the original sources.

How it would work

Which drawings show the ticket office door?

A fictional project question illustrates the intended drawing and contract workflow.

Find the drawings

Start with the floor plan and follow its references to the door schedule and installation detail. The project manager opens the cited sheets and checks their revisions.

Find the contract wording

For a follow-up about defective work, look for the relevant clauses and amendments in the uploaded contract. The answer links to the source wording for review.

Check before acting

The project manager checks the cited sheets. The commercial team reviews any ambiguous obligation or conflict between the documents.

What it is designed to change

Less searching, clearer evidence.

A production pilot would test drawing revision handling, clause citations, project-level access and the time spent finding evidence.

Bring related drawings together

Start with a location or asset and follow references to relevant plans, schedules and details.

Use the project’s contract

Find the relevant clauses and amendments in the documents uploaded for that project.

Trace the answer to the source

Open the original drawing or clause to check the evidence before acting.

Reuse the project index

Keep drawing relationships and document passages available for future questions.
