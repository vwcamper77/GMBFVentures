Privacy

Privacy notice.

How GMBF Ventures Ltd handles personal information sent through this website. Last updated 22 November 2026.

Who we are

GMBF Ventures Ltd is a company registered in England and Wales (Company No. 17286832). Registered office: 124 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX, United Kingdom. We are the controller for personal information you send us through this website. Contact us at hello@gmbfventures.co.uk.

Enquiries: what we collect

When you email us, we receive your email address, message and any other details you include. If an online enquiry form is available, it also asks for your name and enquiry type. Your organisation is optional. We use technical request information to protect online forms against abuse when they are available. Our hosting provider may retain technical logs for security and service operation.

Why we use it

We use your details to handle your enquiry. For general enquiries, we rely on our legitimate interest in responding. If you ask us to take steps towards a contract with you, we use the information needed for those steps. We rely on our legitimate interest in protecting the site and enquiry service to prevent abuse. We need enough detail to understand your enquiry and an email address so we can reply. Please leave out confidential information and other people’s personal details unless they are needed to explain your enquiry.

We do not sell your information, add you to mailing lists or use it for marketing.

Where it is sent

Our mailbox provider stores emails you send us. Our website host is Vercel. When the online enquiry form is available, Vercel receives the form submission and Resend delivers it to our business inbox. These services may process information outside the UK. You can email us to ask about the countries involved, the transfer safeguards that apply and how to obtain a copy.

Cookies and tracking

The public site does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Access controls used for private previews may use cookies.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as we need it to respond and follow up. We may keep it longer where it forms part of a client relationship or is needed for contractual, legal or accounting reasons. Our hosting provider retains technical logs for security and service operation.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights to access, correct and delete your personal information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to data portability. To use any of these rights, email hello@gmbfventures.co.uk. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). How to complain to the ICO.
